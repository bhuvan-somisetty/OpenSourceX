# Product Vision

## What OpenSourceX is

OpenSourceX is an intelligence layer for navigating open source. It connects
mentorship programs, organizations, projects, repositories, people,
communities, issues, contributions, learning and interview practice, and shows
where every fact came from.

## Why it exists

Finding a project to contribute to means combining program websites, GitHub,
project docs and a chatbot by hand. The pieces are disconnected, often
outdated, and rarely say how much to trust them. Beginners cannot tell what is
real, current or right for them.

## Who it serves

Beginners looking for a first project; students preparing for GSoC or LFX
Mentorship; experienced developers evaluating a codebase; contributors who
must explain their own work; and mentors and maintainers who want prepared
applicants.

## What makes it different

1. **Connected ecosystem.** Program → organization → project → repository →
   issues/PRs → learning, in one navigable model.
2. **Evidence first.** Every claim carries source, tier, and last-verified
   date. Conflicts are shown, not hidden.
3. **Honest about unknowns.** Unannounced programs are "not confirmed";
   unverifiable mentors are "not verified".
4. **Understanding, not pretending.** AI teaches and questions; it does not
   write contributions to make someone look experienced.

## Long-term

The place a person starts to understand an ecosystem, choose where to
contribute, learn the project, contribute, and explain that contribution.
Non-goals: social network, autonomous coding agents, autonomous PR submission.
