# Code for Good Tech (C4GT)

C4GT is an ecosystem initiative that bridges the gap between Digital Public Goods and Tech-for-Good builder organizations and talent, particularly students and young professionals. The program creates structured pathways for meaningful development contributions to technology products built for social good.

**How It Works 🛠️**
1. **[Join the Badal Platform:](https://pl-app.iiit.ac.in/c4gt/signup)** Sign up on C4GT Badal using GitHub and Discord, complete your profile, and start exploring projects to contribute to the Tech for Good ecosystem.✨
2. **[Choose Your Project:](https://pl-app.iiit.ac.in/c4gt/projects)** Find a project that matches your skill level and interests from our Open community projects list on Badal Platform. 🔍
3. **Contribute & Collaborate:** Dive into solving issues, submit pull requests, work with mentors to merge pull requests and create impact!  💻


**Initiatives by C4GT**

- >**Dedicated Mentoring Program (DMP):** The Dedicated Mentoring Program (DMP) is C4GT's flagship structured mentorship initiative that connects talented contributors with Tech-for-Good organizations to build production-ready solutions for real-world challenges under the guidance of experienced mentors from partner organizations and the C4GT mentor network. Over three months, contributors gain hands-on open-source experience while earning a stipend, and participating organizations benefit from dedicated contributor capacity, structured project support and monitoring, and 50% stipend co-funding from C4GT to accelerate the delivery of their product roadmaps.
  
- >**C4GT Hubs:** A multi-year talent development initiative that builds a sustainable pipeline from learning to employment - following a hub-and-spoke model with IIIT Hyderabad as the anchor hub and partner colleges as regional spokes.


**Why Contribute to C4GT?** 🌟

- >**Learn & Grow:** Enhance your technical skills and gain exposure to impactful DPG projects. 📚
- >**Network & Connect:** Collaborate with a diverse community of developers, mentors, and industry leaders. 🤝
- >**Make an Impact:** Contribute to open-source projects that make a difference at a population scale. 🌍
