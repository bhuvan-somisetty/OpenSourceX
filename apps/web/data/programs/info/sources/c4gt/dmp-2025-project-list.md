Do you want to turbocharge your career by contributing to live open-source projects with real-world social impact, enhance your tech skills, receive 1:1 mentorship from domain experts, network with global open-source leaders, all while earning a stipend of Rs. 1 lakh over 3 months? If yes, look no further, Code for GovTech's Dedicated Mentoring Program (DMP) is the fit for you. You can go through the projects listed here and follow instructions listed on [Apna](https://apna.co/contests/dmp_2025) to apply. Applications shut 5th May.

Do not ask process-related questions about how to apply and who to contact in the tickets listed below. The only questions allowed are about technical aspects of the project itself. If you want help with the process, you can refer to the instructions listed on Apna, and any further queries can be taken up on our [Discord channel](https://discord.com/invite/VPrXf7Jxpr) titled DMP queries.
