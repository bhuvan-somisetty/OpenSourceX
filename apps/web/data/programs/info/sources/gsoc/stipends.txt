Contributor Stipends for 2026

Page Summary
outlined_flag
GSoC stipends are paid in two installments after successful evaluations, with 45% disbursed midway and 55% at the project's end.
Stipend amounts vary based on project size (small, medium, large) and are adjusted by Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) based on the student's location.
Students can find their specific stipend amount by referencing the comprehensive table listing amounts for various countries, categorized by project size.
Small projects range from $750 to $1650, medium projects range from $1500 to $3300, and large projects range from $3000 to $6600 USD, depending on location.
Stipends are paid in two parts, one after each successful evaluation. The
dates below are based on the standard 12 week project. If your project is
longer, your evaluation and payments schedule will be different. The first
evaluation will occur at the halfway point of your project (e.g. For a 16
week project the evaluation will occur after week 8). You can also check
your GSoC dashboard to view when your evaluations occur.
First Evaluation (paid July 11): 45%
Final Evaluation (paid September 1): 55%
Stipend amounts are calculated based on your location.
How is my location determined?
Your location is determined by the country where you are residing during
the GSoC 2026 coding period.
How are the stipend amounts determined?
We are using a Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) based calculation to
determine the stipend.
For medium sized projects: We start with a base amount of 3000 USD and
then adjust it based on each country's PPP value. There is a minimum (1500
USD) and maximum (3300 USD) stipend amount.
For large sized projects: We start with a base amount of 6000 USD and then
adjust it based on each country's PPP value. There is a minimum (3000 USD)
and maximum (6600 USD) stipend amount.
For small sized projects: Google starts with a base amount of 1500 USD and
then adjust it based on each country's PPP value. There is a minimum (750
USD) and maximum (1650 USD) stipend amount.
What is Purchasing Power Parity? (PPP)
Purchasing Power Parity is a way to estimate the exchange rate between two
currencies to determine equivalent purchasing power.
Wikipedia: Purchasing Power Parity
World Bank: Fundamentals of Purchasing Power Parity
If I don't see my country listed, can I still be paid?
Our global payment provider, Payoneer, services 190+ countries around the
world. If you don't see your country listed below, you are not eligible to
receive a payment for your participation in GSoC.
A special note about Canada: Due to local regulatory requirements, effective
in 2026 Payoneer will no longer be able to provide payment disbursements and
other payment services to Payoneer account holders registered or residing in
the province of Quebec, Canada. This affects new and existing Payoneer account
holders with address and/or the place of incorporation in Quebec.
