## How to get selected for GSoC

1. **Pick 1–3 organizations** from the accepted list (on the GSoC site, or the [GSoC page on OpenSourceX](/programs/gsoc)). Read their **project ideas** lists.
2. **Join their chat or mailing list early** and introduce yourself. Ask focused questions about the idea you like.
3. **Contribute before the proposal period:** set up the project, fix a good first issue, and get a PR reviewed or merged. Most organizations expect it, and some require it.
4. **Write the proposal** (see *Writing a mentorship proposal*). Share a draft with the mentors and revise it based on their feedback.
5. **Submit up to three proposals** on summerofcode.withgoogle.com before the deadline. Only one can be accepted.
6. Organizations rank proposals, and Google announces the accepted contributors on the published date.

## GSoC prerequisites and eligibility

- 18 or older at registration.
- A student, or an open source beginner (roughly fewer than 10 PRs to open source).
- Eligible to work in your country of residence.
- Accepted into GSoC no more than once before.
- Not resident in a U.S.-embargoed country.

There is no formal certificate or cover-letter upload like LFX. The **proposal is the application**, and many organizations also ask for a merged PR or a solved qualification task. Check each organization's "how to apply" or "contributor guidance" page, which OpenSourceX links on the organization's page when it is recorded.

## GSoC project sizes and stipend

Projects are about 90, 175 or 350 hours, over 8 to 22 weeks. The stipend depends on the project size and is adjusted by country (Purchasing Power Parity). Ask "GSoC stipend" for the recorded amounts.

## GSoC tips

- Choose an organization whose code you can actually run and understand. A smaller, well-documented project beats a famous one.
- Quality over quantity: one excellent proposal with PRs behind it beats three generic ones.
- Use the organization's own proposal template if it has one.
- Be visible but polite in the chat: answer other newcomers' questions too.
