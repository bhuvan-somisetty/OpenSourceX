## Difference between GSoC, LFX, Outreachy and other programs (comparison)

| Program | Who it is for | When | How you apply | Paid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [LFX Mentorship](/programs/lfx) | Students and professionals | 3 terms a year (Mar–May, Jun–Aug, Sep–Nov) | Resume + cover letter + project prerequisites, up to 3 programs per term | Yes, by country |
| [Google Summer of Code](/programs/gsoc) | Students and open source beginners, 18+ | Once a year, in summer | Up to 3 proposals; one can be accepted | Yes, by project size and country |
| [Outreachy](/programs/outreachy) | People under-represented in tech | May–Aug and Dec–Mar | Essays, then a contribution period, then a final application | Yes, the same everywhere |
| [Summer of Bitcoin](/programs/summer-of-bitcoin) | University and high school students | Summer, 12 weeks | Challenge bootcamp, then a proposal | Yes, in bitcoin |
| [European Summer of Code](/programs/esoc) | Contributors to European projects | Summer | See the program page | Yes |
| [Code for GovTech (C4GT)](/programs/c4gt) | Contributors in India | Yearly | See the program page | Yes |
| [MLH Fellowship](/programs/mlh-fellowship) | Students | Several batches a year | Application and interview | Varies |

See every program on the [Programs page](/programs).

## Which program should I pick?

- **New to open source:** GSoC (designed for beginners) or LFX projects marked beginner-friendly.
- **Cloud native or Kubernetes:** LFX Mentorship, where CNCF projects run every term.
- **Under-represented in tech:** Outreachy.
- **Already did GSoC:** check eligibility first. Outreachy excludes earlier GSoC interns, and some programs limit repeat participation. Plan the order so you don't lock yourself out.
- You can prepare for several, but the timelines overlap, and you can usually take part in only one at a time.
