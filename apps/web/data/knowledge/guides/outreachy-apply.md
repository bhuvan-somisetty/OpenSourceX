## Outreachy application steps

1. **Initial application** with eligibility questions and essays about the bias or discrimination you face in tech in your country. Generative AI is **not allowed** for the essays.
2. If approved, the **contribution period** begins. Pick up to two projects, follow the mentors' instructions, and **record at least one contribution** on the Outreachy site.
3. Submit a **final application** for that project, including a project timeline.
4. Mentors pick the interns, and results are announced on the published date.

## Outreachy prerequisites and eligibility

- 18 or older by the internship start date.
- You face under-representation, systemic bias or discrimination in tech in your country.
- Never an Outreachy, Outreach Program for Women or GSoC intern before.
- No full-time job, contract or other internship during the internship. Part-time jobs need organizer approval.
- Students need 42 consecutive days free of classes and exams during the internship. Students in India count as Northern Hemisphere, so they can only apply for the May–August cohort.
- 30 hours a week for 13 weeks, remote. The stipend is the same in every country (ask "Outreachy stipend").

## Outreachy tips

- Start the contribution period early, and record every contribution, not just the merged ones.
- Ask questions in the project's public channel and help other applicants.
- Base the final-application timeline on what you learned during the contribution period.
