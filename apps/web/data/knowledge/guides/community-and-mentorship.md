## How to ask good questions in a project's chat

- Ask in **public channels**, not DMs, so the answer helps the next person too.
- Say what you tried, what you expected, and what happened, with logs or error text in code blocks.
- Search the issues, docs and channel history first.
- Don't ask to ask. Post the question directly.
- Be patient and thank people. Maintainers are busy, and many are volunteers.
- Follow the project's code of conduct.

## Succeeding during a mentorship

- Agree on goals, a weekly meeting time and how you will report progress in week 1.
- Send short **weekly updates**: done, next, blocked.
- Open **small, reviewable PRs** early instead of one huge PR at the end.
- Raise blockers within a day or two. Don't go silent.
- LFX evaluations come every few weeks, and the **midterm and final are required** to receive each half of the stipend (ask "LFX stipend").
- Write a short summary or blog post at the end. It helps your future applications.

## After the mentorship: from mentee to maintainer

Many mentees keep contributing, become reviewers, then co-mentor the next term, and some become maintainers. For example, more than half of the CNCF's 2025 mentees were still contributing a year later. Keep the momentum: pick the follow-up issues from your project, review others' PRs, and join community meetings.
